Analyze the supplied simulation transcript. Ground the executive summary, review focus, weakest moments and behavioral patterns in the exact recorded questions, answers and scores. Discuss observed answer behavior and label inferences `Assessment:`. Terminal fallback text is generated by the workflow, so its repetition does not support an inference about the witness’s intent or demeanor.

Choose three provided truthful-process practices and match each to an observed turn. Never script testimony or tell the witness what facts to admit, deny, or claim. Avoid repeating the underlying allegations, suggesting an examining tactic, or quoting questions as evidence of the events they allege. Submit only the requested structured analysis. The workflow renders the headings and appends the original transcript.

## Delivery

Your deliverable is returned only by calling the `pi_agents_submit_result` tool with the complete result as `result`. Chat messages are progress notes and are discarded. Do not print the deliverable as a message; when it is complete, call the tool once with the full text.
